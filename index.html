THE OBJECTIVE
Bring at least 8 of 12 prisoners home in six minutes. One escapee crosses at a time; the next leaves after rescue or loss. Protect your gun and identify targets before firing.

THE OPPOSITION
Raiders focus on the exposed escapee. Sappers rush your gun. Machine-gun squads approach from the upper left: COVER for their burst, then GO during the green reload window. Destroying a squad buys time before reinforcements. A bark warns of a pursuing dog: shelter the escapee, then clear it before GO. Dogs take one hit.
